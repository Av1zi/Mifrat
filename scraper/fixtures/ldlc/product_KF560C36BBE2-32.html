Overview

Kingston FURY Beast DDR5

Kingston FURY Beast DDR5 memory brings the latest cutting-edge technology to next-generation gaming platforms. Pushing speed, capacity and reliability even further, DDR5 comes with an arsenal of enhanced features, such as on-chip ECC (ODECC) for greater stability at extreme speeds, dual 32-bit subchannels for increased efficiency, and a range of new features.32-bit dual subchannels for increased efficiency, and a module power management integrated circuit (PMIC) to deliver power where it's needed most.

Whether you're pushing the limits of your games with the most extreme settings, streaming live 4K+ or delivering big animations and 3D renders, the Kingston FURY Beast DDR5 is the next level you need, combining style and performance. What's more, it uses AMD EXPO, a new overclocking specification that includes two customisable profiles for speeds and timings.

Kingston FURY Beast DDR5

  • Compatible AMD EXPO
  • Tested and proven so you can build and upgrade with confidence on your favourite motherboard.
  • The new heatsink combines bold styling with prodigious cooling functionality.
  • With double the banks and two independent 32-bit sub-channels, DDR5 data management excels in the latest and most demanding games, programs and applications.
