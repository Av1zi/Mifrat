Overview

Corsair Vengeance RGB RS: DDR4 ARGB RAM with exceptional performance

Corsair Vengeance RGB RS DDR4 RAM enhances the aesthetics of your PC while delivering exceptional performance. A custom PCB delivers high signal quality for exceptional performance and stability on the latest Intel and AMD DDR4 motherboards, while slice-and-dice memory chips unlock superior overclocking potential. Illuminate your system with dynamic RGB lighting from six individually addressable LEDs.

With Corsair iCUE software you take control and customize the ARGB lighting to match your setup. Create custom lighting profiles and sync with all iCUE-compatible devices throughout the iCUE ecosystem.

Aluminium radiator and custom ARGB backlight

  • Carefully selected memory chips for high overclocking potential
  • Take control with Corsair iCUE software and synchronize lighting with other Corsair RGB products throughout the iCUE ecosystem.
  • Illuminate your system with dynamic six-LED ARGB lighting
  • Choose from dozens of preset lighting profiles or create your own
  • Optimized for exceptional performance on the latest Intel and AMD DDR4 motherboards
  • Aluminum heat sink that conducts heat away from your memory
